Membership Benefits

Savings & Credit

Access to competitive interest rates on savings and affordable credit facilities for personal and business needs.

Business Support

Comprehensive business services including loans, insurance, and advisory support for entrepreneurial ventures.

Consumer Services

Access to consumer durables, essential goods, and services at member-friendly prices and terms.

Training & Development

Regular workshops, skill development programs, and educational initiatives to enhance member capabilities.

Community Network

Connect with like-minded individuals and businesses in our cooperative community network.

Voting Rights

Participate in society governance through voting rights and representation in decision-making processes.

Membership Information

Eligibility

  • Must be a resident of our operational areas
  • Minimum age of 18 years
  • Sound financial standing
  • Commitment to cooperative principles

Membership Process

  1. Submit application form with required documents
  2. Pay initial membership fee and share capital
  3. Attend orientation session
  4. Receive membership certificate and member ID

Required Documents

  • Identity proof (Aadhaar Card/PAN Card)
  • Passport size photographs (2 nos.)
